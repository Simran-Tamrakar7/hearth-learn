## Stakeholder UAT Process

Engage business domain experts with clear business task scenarios and formal sign-off gates.

```
UAT Scenario: Monthly Payroll Disbursal Verification
1. Log in as Payroll Manager
2. Verify gross and tax amounts
3. Export sign-off report
```
