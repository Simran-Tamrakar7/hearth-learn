Spike testing is a sudden sharp jump in load — not the gradual ramp of load testing (Chapter 14) and not climbing past capacity to find a ceiling (Chapter 15).

## Jump, hold, drop, recover

A multi-minute climb is load; raising the peak to find a ceiling is stress.
