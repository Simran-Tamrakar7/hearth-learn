overviewText: "Conformance testing verifies that an application's output — typically HTML/CSS markup, but potentially any defined protocol or specification — actually conforms to a formal external standard, checked directly against that standard's own official validator rather than against internal expectations of what \"correct\" looks like."
why: "Code can render correctly in the one or two browsers a team happens to test in while still silently violating the underlying standard those browsers are simply being lenient about — technical debt that surfaces later as unpredictable rendering in a browser or tool that isn't as forgiving, as an accessibility failure, or simply as markup that's harder to maintain than it needs to be. Conformance testing catches that gap directly and early, against an authoritative, objective standard rather than \"did it look fine in Chrome.\""
when: "Periodically across development, and specifically after any significant markup/template change — cheap and fast enough to run routinely rather than saved only for a final pre-release check."
